## QUICK REFERENCE: WHAT EACH CONTENT TYPE NEEDS

### Video Scripts (60-sec, 30-sec, 90-sec)

**Essential Elements:**
- Hook (first 3 seconds): Pain point, not solution
- Transformation: Show before/after or problem/solution
- Feature demo: Real dashboard/workflow shown

- CTA: Clickable link or phone number

### Paid Ad Copy (LinkedIn, Facebook, Google)

**Formula:**
1. **Headline:** Problem statement (not solution)
2. **Pain point:** Specific frustration (40 hours/week wasted, invoices manual, compliance stress)
3. **Solution teaser:** What changes
4. **Results:** Specific metric
5. **CTA:** "Book demo" or "Start trial"

**Character limits:**
- LinkedIn: 300-500 chars (headline) + body
- Facebook: 125 char headline, 27 char CTA button
- Google Ads: 30 char headline, 90 char description

---

### Email Sequences

**Structure:**
- **Email 1 (Day 0):** Welcome + one main benefit
- **Email 2 (Day 3):** Feature spotlight + how-to
- **Email 3 (Day 7):** Results from early adopters + upgrade CTA
- **Email 4 (Day 14):** Customer success story + final CTA

**Tone:** Helpful, not pushy. Problem-aware. Specific.

### Landing Pages

**Sections:**
1. **Hero:** Problem + solution (above fold)
2. **Problem:** Detailed pain points (40+ hours detail)
3. **Solution:** 4-6 key features with visuals
4. **Results:** ROI calculation, metrics
5. **Social Proof:** Testimonials, case studies
6. **FAQ:** Objection handling
7. **CTA:** "Start free trial" or "Schedule demo"

### Blog Posts

**Format (1,500-2,000 words):**
1. **Intro:** Hook with problem
2. **Section 1:** Industry context (why this matters)
3. **Section 2:** Traditional solution (manual processes, their costs)
4. **Section 3:** Modern solution (what changed)
5. **Section 4:** How ACG solves it (specific features)
6. **Section 5:** Real example/case study
7. **Conclusion:** What's possible now
8. **CTA:** "See it in action"

---

### Case Studies

**Format (1,000-1,500 words):**
1. **Company snapshot:** Industry, size, location
2. **Challenge:** Specific problems before
3. **Discovery:** How they found ACG
4. **Implementation:** Weeks 1-4 timeline
5. **Results:** Numbers + timeline ("Month 1: X, Month 3: Y")
6. **Testimonial:** Quote from decision-maker
7. **Lessons learned:** What worked, what to watch for
8. **What's next:** Their future plans

✅ Use specific numbers ("15 minutes" not "faster")
✅ Start with problem, not solution
✅ Acknowledge objections head-on
✅ Use healthcare jargon appropriately
✅ Show empathy for current pain

❌ Exaggerate performance (mark projections as such)

❌ Make claims without data

## OBJECTION HANDLING FRAMEWORK

When AI encounters objections, use this formula:

**Pattern:** [Objection] → [Reframe] → [Evidence] → [Reassurance] → [Next Step]

### Objection 1: "We've tried software before. It didn't work."
**Reframe:** Those were dashboards. ACG is autonomous AI.
**Evidence:** Dashboard requires manual work. ACG automates 95% of routine tasks.
**Reassurance:** 30-day free trial; most agencies know within 2 weeks if it works.
**Next Step:** "See it live with your data. Zero obligation."

### Objection 2: "Will our staff accept being managed by AI?"
**Reframe:** Staff control everything. AI respects their preferences.
**Evidence:** 70%+ acceptance rate in trials; staff actually prefer convenience.
**Reassurance:** Staff set availability, preferences, notification times. AI makes offers they can refuse.
**Next Step:** "See staff experience in demo."

### Objection 3: "Is our data safe?"
**Reframe:** We treat security like healthcare providers do (because we are).
**Evidence:** GDPR-compliant, encrypted at rest/in transit, multi-tenant isolation, full audit logs.
**Reassurance:** CQC-audit-ready infrastructure; we've passed security audits with other healthcare clients.
**Next Step:** "Introduce you to our security team or another client."

### Objection 4: "The cost seems high."
**Reframe:** Cost of NOT doing it is higher.
**Evidence:** 2 admin staff @ £28k = £56k. Lost revenue @ 25% miss rate = £40k. Manual processes = £20k.
**Reassurance:** ROI typically 6-8 weeks; after that, pure profit.
**Next Step:** "Run the ROI calculator with your specific numbers."

### Objection 5: "Will the AI make mistakes?"
**Reframe:** AI makes fewer mistakes than humans. Mistakes are caught and flagged.
**Evidence:** <1% error rate; confidence scoring means 98%+ of auto-decisions are correct.
**Reassurance:** High-confidence decisions auto-execute; uncertain decisions flag for human review.
**Next Step:** "See the confidence scoring system in demo."
