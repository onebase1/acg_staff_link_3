To qualify, you generally need to tick all these boxes:[^4_3][^4_4][^4_9][^4_10][^4_2][^4_1]

- **Personal:**
    - 18 or over.
    - Current UK resident.
    - Right to live and work in the UK for the duration of the loan.
- **Business:**
    - New business or trading **less than 36 months** (you fit this as early‑stage SaaS).[^4_4][^4_2][^4_3][^4_1]
    - Business is based in the UK (your agencies are UK‑focused, so ✓).[^4_2][^4_3][^4_1]
    - Genuine business need for the funds with a clear plan (your £10k asset plan does this).[^4_10][^4_4]
    - You self‑declare that you **can’t access traditional bank finance** easily (common for early SaaS founders).[^4_7][^4_2]
- **Financial/Checks:**
    - Personal credit check – they assess affordability and any serious issues (bankruptcy, IVA, DRO would block approval).[^4_4][^4_7]
    - Loan must not be used to refinance existing personal debts; it must fund growth (your use for product/video/UI/marketing assets is eligible).[^4_8][^4_4]
- **What you’ll need for the application:**[^4_5][^4_4]
    - Business plan and 12–24 month cashflow forecast (templates are provided).
    - Breakdown of how you’ll use the £10k (you already have this: video, UI, landing, deck, contingency).
    - Basic market research and explanation of your customers (home‑care/agency owners).
    - Personal documents: ID, proof of address, bank statements.

## How to describe what your app does (loan \& investor friendly)

Use a short, repeatable pitch that explains: **who**, **what problem**, **how you’re different**, **why now**.

### One‑liner

> “AgileCare Management is a plug‑and‑play **operating system** for care and support agencies that runs day‑to‑day operations autonomously using embedded AI, so owners spend almost no time on admin.”

### Problem statement (short)

- Small and mid‑sized agencies are drowning in **manual admin**: rostering, compliance, visit notes, billing, messaging, incident reporting.
- Existing “core systems” are old, clunky, and **don’t fully automate workflows**; owners still rely heavily on spreadsheets, phone calls, and extra staff.
- Most AI tools are sold as **add‑ons or integrations**, so the owner ends up with multiple systems glued together instead of one intelligent platform.


### Your solution (short)

- You **rebuilt the core agency system from scratch** with AI and automations **embedded into the workflows**, not bolted on afterwards.
- Instead of “AI on the side”, the app behaves like a smart operations manager: predicting schedules, chasing tasks, generating documents, and handling routine decisions automatically.
- It’s plug‑and‑play for both **new** and **existing** agency owners; they can move from spreadsheets/legacy systems and get automation from day one.


### Why it’s different (your edge)

You can phrase it like this in applications/conversations:

- “Unlike tools that just sell AI integrations or ‘agents’ on top of legacy software, AgileCare behaves as an **AI‑embedded operating system**. Owners don’t buy an ‘AI tool’; they switch to a system where admin is largely done for them.”
- “I’m not competing with AI automation vendors—I’m competing directly with existing core system providers by offering a next‑generation platform that automates 80–90% of the operational workload.”
- “Only a tiny fraction of small businesses have truly adopted AI agents in their day‑to‑day operations; most are stuck with static software plus manual work. AgileCare closes that gap for care agencies by baking AI into the core product from day one.”

(You can reference that adoption of AI in small business is still very low in general terms, but avoid quoting a precise ‘2%’ stat in official docs unless you have a source; instead say “a small minority” or “a small percentage”.)

### Proof and traction (very important for loan \& equity)

- MVP built and in **production testing**.
- **4 agencies already onboarded** (or committed as tenants) – shows real demand and reduces risk.
- The £10k will go specifically into:
    - Polished UI for key workflows.
    - Explainer and demo videos to close more agencies.
    - A clearer landing page and pitch deck to support growth and investment.

This tells loan assessors: “The tech is built; you’re funding **go‑to‑market and polish**, not an idea on paper.”
