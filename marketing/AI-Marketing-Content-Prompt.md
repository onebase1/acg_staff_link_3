1. **Tone:** Professional but approachable; confident but not arrogant
2. **Vocabulary:** Use healthcare/agency jargon when appropriate; explain technical terms
3. **Storytelling:** Lead with problem (ops manager's frustration), show solution (AI handling it), deliver results (time saved, revenue gained)
4. **Visual Style:** Clean, modern dashboard screenshots; real-world scenarios; before/after comparisons
5. **Authenticity:** Use real data from trials; avoid exaggeration; always include disclaimers for performance claims

### Quality Standards
- **Accuracy:** Every claim must be verifiable from the app or documentation
- **Specificity:** Use concrete numbers (15 min, 98%, 40 hours) not vague claims
- **Objection-Ready:** Anticipate skepticism; provide evidence and reassurance
- **CTA-Focused:** Every piece of content should drive toward one of three actions: demo, trial, or trial signup
