# Presentation Speech & Pitch Prep

## 1. The Core "One-Liner"
*   **The Hook**: "We're automating the chaos of care staffing so owners can stop being firefighters and start being directors."
*   **The Transformation**: "ACG StaffLink is an AI-powered operations center that handles everything from 7 AM call-offs to compliance and invoicing, automatically."

## 2. Competitive Edge (The "Moat")
*   **Agency Response**: "Most software is just a digital filing cabinet. We built an active team member. Our system doesn't just store data; it makes decisions—matching staff by reliability, not just proximity."
*   **Technical Edge**: "We've orchestrated a proprietary AI layer (n8n + Supabase) that handles the high-friction automation that legacy software ignores. We're competing directly with core systems, not just selling AI bolt-ons."

## 3. Audience-Specific Talk Tracks

### A. For Agency Owners (Sales)
> "You didn't start this business to spend your weekends chasing timesheets or panicking over an expired DBS. ACG handles the 'admin debt' that kills your growth. We give you back 20 hours a week and 100% audit peace of mind."

### B. For Potential Partners (Vision)
> "We've validated the MVP with 4+ agencies and the tech is proven. We're positioned to dominate the North East staffing market by being the first truly AI-first platform. I'm looking for a partner to move from 'Production Testing' to 'Market Conquest'. We're offering a 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff to ensure long-term alignment."

### C. For Grant Boards & Competitions (Innovation)
> "ACG StaffLink is solving a critical healthcare bottleneck: staffing inefficiency. By leveraging advanced AI orchestration, we're reducing the time-to-fill for urgent shifts from hours to minutes. We are a North East high-growth tech project targeting a £200B+ global market, starting right here in Newcastle."

*   **The Deal**: "We're offering a SAFE note with an 8-12% conversion or a £100k valuation cap. This is SEIS-eligible, giving you 50% tax relief on your investment."

## 5. Objection Handling

*   **"Is it just another CRM?"**
    *   *Answer*: "No. A CRM stores contacts. We book shifts. If a document expires, we don't just alert you—the system automatically suspends that user from the marketplace. It's active protection, not just passive tracking."
*   **"Is the AI reliable?"**
    *   *Answer*: "We use a 'Conversational Verification' model. The AI handles the initial triage and routine bookings, but human oversight is baked into every major decision point."
