## VIDEO 4: MAIN DEMO - "OPERATIONS ON AUTOPILOT" (4:30 Minutes)
**Style Guide (The "Clay" Aesthetic):**
*   **Motion:** High frame-rate (60fps) smooth cursor movements. Easing (Bezier curves) on all UI transitions.
*   **Focus:** Usage of heavy depth-of-field (Gaussian Blur) to de-emphasize background when focusing on specific UI elements (Smart Match button, Compliance status).
*   **Typography:** Large, bold, kinetic text overlays for key metrics ("98%", "15 MIN").
*   **Pacing:** Rhythmic editing. Cuts should land on the beat of the background track (Driving, percussive lo-fi or modern tech house).

**Section 1: Opening - The Problem (0:00-0:30)**
**Audio:** "Running a care agency means juggling a thousand moving pieces. Staffing gaps. Compliance documents expiring. Hours lost to admin just to stay afloat. The bigger you grow, the wook rse the chaos gets. You didn't start this business to be a firefighter."
**Visual:**
*   **Effect:** Split screen with a sharp divider.
*   **Left (The Old Way):** Desaturated, lower frame rate footage of a stressed manager. Overlay frantic "Missed Call" notifications stacking up rapidly.
*   **Right (The Goal):** Blur placeholder.
*   **Action:** As audio says "firefighter", screen swipes completely to black with a single, glowing red notification icon pulsing.

**Section 2: Product Introduction (0:30-1:00)**
**Audio:** "This is ACG StaffLink. It’s not just a database. It is an AI-powered operations center that actively manages your agency. It automates the work your team shouldn't be doing, so they can focus on what matters—your clients."
**Visual:**
*   **Transition:** The pulsing red icon *morphs* smoothly into the blue 'A' logo of ACG StaffLink.
*   **Reveal:** Zoom out to reveal the full Dashboard in Dark Mode. Glassmorphism panels slide into place.
*   **Kinetic Text:** Words "ACTIVE MEMBER" scale up on screen.
*   **Stats:** "98% Fill Rate" card pops out (3D float effect) and number counts up rapidly from 0 to 98.

**Section 3: Emergency Staffing (1:00-1:45)**
**Audio:** "Let’s look at a Monday morning nightmare. A call-off comes in at 7 AM. Usually, this means panic. With ACG, you simply mark the shift as 'Urgent'. Instantly, our 'Smart Match' algorithm broadcasts to your top 142 eligible staff. One click. Average fill time? 15 minutes."
**Visual:**
*   **Cursor:** Smooth, high-contrast cursor glides to "Create Shift".
*   **Click:** Ripple effect on click. Toggles "Urgent" switch -> Switch turns neon red.
*   **Broadcast:** Map view explodes with 142 plotted points (Staff) turning from grey to blue (Notified).
*   **Mobile Mockup:** iPhone 15 frame slides smoothly into view. Notification "URGENT SHIFT" slides down. Thumb taps "Accept".
*   **Metric:** Large counter overlay: "TIME TO FILL: 14:59" counting down rapidly to "00:00" as status turns Green.

**Section 4: Compliance Automation (1:45-2:30)**
**Audio:** "Compliance is your license to operate. ACG StaffLink guards it like a fortress. Our system scans every single document, for every staff member, every morning at 8 AM. It sends 30-day warnings automatically. And if a document expires? It auto-suspends the staff member instantly. Zero violations. Guaranteed."
**Visual:**
*   **Scanning Effect:** List of staff names scrolling rapidly. A horizontal "Laser Scan" line moves down the list.
*   **Alert:** One name highlights Red.
*   **Zoom:** Camera snap-zooms into the specific document row: "DBS Check: Expired".
*   **Automation:** Shows the "Status" toggle flipping automatically from "Active" to "Suspended" with a padlock animation locking into place.
*   **Text Overlay:** "ZERO RISK" stamped on screen in heavy bold font.

**Section 5: Billing, Matching & Visibility (2:30-4:20)**
**Audio:** "And finally, the back office. Invoicing is automated—approved timesheets turn into draft invoices in one click. Staff are intelligently matched based on reliability scores, not just who answers the phone. You get real-time visibility into everything. This is operations on autopilot."
**Visual:**
*   **Split View:** Left side shows Timesheet, Right side shows Invoice.
*   **Morph:** As "One click" is spoken, the Timesheet *morphs* seamlessly into the Invoice PDF.
*   **Moneyball:** Staff profile card moves to center. "Reliability Score: 98%" circle chart fills up. Map pins pop up showing "5 min away".
*   **Dashboard:** Final shot of the main dashboard with all graphs trending upward (Green).

**Closing (4:20-4:30)**
**Audio:** "Your team focuses on client relationships, staff development, and growth. We handle the rest. ACG StaffLink. Operations on Autopilot."
**Visual:**
*   **Zoom Out:** Slow, cinematic zoom out from the screen interface to a lifestyle shot of a relaxed Director (referencing Section 1).
*   **End Card:** Logo on black background. "Book Transformation" button glows. URL fades in.
