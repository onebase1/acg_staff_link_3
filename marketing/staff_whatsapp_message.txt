📢 *Compliance Update: What to expect when documents are rejected*

Hi Team,

We’ve improved how we communicate compliance feedback to make it easier for you to stay compliant without cluttering your inbox! 🚀

*What to expect moving forward:*
Instead of receiving multiple separate emails, you will now receive a **single summary email** listing all documents that need attention. This email will group everything together and provide clear reasons from the admin (e.g., blurred photo, expired date).

*Note:* You do **not** need to check your portal immediately. This is just to let you know how notifications will work going forward. 

*If you do receive an "Action Required" email in the future:*
1. Log into the *Staff Portal* and head to the *Compliance Tracker*.
2. Look for items with a *'Rejected'* badge.
3. Click *'Edit'* to see the specific feedback and upload a new clear copy.

Once you upload a replacement, your progress will show as *'Awaiting Review'*. Thanks for helping us keep our service standards high! ⏱️
