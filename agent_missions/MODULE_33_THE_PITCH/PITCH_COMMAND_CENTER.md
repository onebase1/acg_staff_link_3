# SECTION 5: OBJECTION HANDLING SCRIPTS

## 5.1 Common Objections & Responses

### "We're happy with our current system"

> "That's great—sounds like things are working. Out of curiosity, how long does it typically take to fill an urgent shift?
>
> *[If they give a number > 5 minutes]*
>
> What would it mean for your operation if that dropped to under 2 minutes? That's the difference we're talking about."

---

### "We've tried software before, it didn't work"

> "I hear that a lot. Most staffing software asks staff to download an app, log in, check portals. That's why it fails—staff don't use it.
>
> We work through WhatsApp. Your staff already have it. No training, no new app, no passwords. That's why adoption is near 100%."

---

### "My staff won't use technology"

> "That's exactly why we built this on WhatsApp. Your staff already send messages every day. They don't need to learn anything new.
>
> They get one message: 'Shift available at [location] on [date]. Reply YES to accept.' That's it. If they can reply to a message, they can use this."

---

### "It's too expensive"

> "Let me ask you this: how many shifts do you miss each month because you can't reach staff fast enough?
>
> If it's even 2 shifts at £300 each, that's £600/month—£7,200/year in lost revenue.
>
> Our system costs a fraction of that. It pays for itself in the first month."

---

### "I don't trust AI to make decisions"

> "I completely understand. Here's the thing: the AI doesn't make decisions on its own. It follows rules YOU set.
>
> You decide who's eligible for which shifts. You decide compliance requirements. The AI just executes faster than a human could.
>
> And for anything that needs judgment? It routes to a human. You stay in control."

---

### "We need to think about it"

> "Of course. Can I ask—what specifically do you need to think through?
>
> *[Listen]*
>
> If those concerns were addressed, would you be ready to try a pilot?"

---

### "Can it integrate with our existing system?"

> "Great question. ACG StaffLink is designed to sit alongside your existing tools, not replace them.
>
> We can import your staff data, sync with your calendar, and export to your invoicing system. The goal is to add automation without disrupting what already works."

---

### "What if it doesn't work for us?"

> "That's exactly why I'm proposing a pilot, not a contract.
>
> 60 days. A subset of your staff. If it works, you'll see the results in the numbers. If it doesn't, you walk away. No obligation.
>
> But I'm confident you'll see the difference in the first week."

---
