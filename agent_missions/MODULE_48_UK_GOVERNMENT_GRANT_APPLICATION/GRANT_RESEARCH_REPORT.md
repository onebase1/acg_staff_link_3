## SECTION 4: SEIS ANGEL INVESTMENT

### Overview
- **Type:** Tax-advantaged equity investment (not grant or loan)
- **Managing Body:** HMRC (Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs)
- **Legislation:** Seed Enterprise Investment Scheme (SEIS)
- **Investor Benefit:** 50% income tax relief + CGT exemption

### Investment Limits

**Per Company:**
- Maximum raise: £250,000 total lifetime
- Must spend within: 3 years of investment
- Eligible purposes: Business growth, R&D ✓ (beta testing, marketing)

**Per Investor:**
- Maximum investment: £200,000 per tax year
- Minimum investment: No legal minimum (typically £5k-£10k)
- Tax relief: 50% of investment back via income tax reduction

### Eligibility Criteria

✅ **Company must be:**
- UK-registered and headquartered ✓ (OneBase Group Ltd)
- Trading for less than 2 years ✓ (just registered)
- Fewer than 25 employees ✓ (1 employee - you)
- Gross assets under £350,000 ✓ (pre-revenue)
- Not listed on stock exchange ✓
- Conducting qualifying trade (software development ✓)

✅ **Investment must be:**
- Used for business growth ✓ (beta testing, marketing)
- Ordinary shares (equity, not loan)
- New shares issued (not purchasing existing shares)
- Not replacing existing investment

❌ **Exclusions:**
- Cannot be used to pay off loans
- Cannot pay dividends to investors for 3 years
- Cannot be "connected" investor (you cannot invest in your own company via SEIS)

### Investor Tax Benefits

**Example: £50,000 Investment**
- **Income Tax Relief (50%):** £25,000 back in tax year of investment
- **Net Investment Cost:** £25,000 (after tax relief)
- **CGT Exemption:** If shares held 3+ years, any gain is tax-free
- **Loss Relief:** If company fails, can claim loss against income tax

**Example Scenario:**
- Investor earns £100k/year, pays £30k income tax
- Invests £50k in OneBase Group via SEIS
- Claims £25k income tax relief (pays only £5k tax that year)
- If OneBase exits at £500k valuation (10x return):
  - Investor's shares worth £500k
  - No capital gains tax (normally 20% = £90k)
  - Net profit: £475k on £25k net investment (19x return!)

**Why Investors Love SEIS:**
- Government effectively co-invests 50%
- Downside protected (loss relief if fail)
- Upside unlimited (CGT-free)

### Application Process

**Step 1: HMRC Advance Assurance (Weeks 1-8)**
- Apply to HMRC confirming SEIS eligibility
- Submit form SEIS1 (Advance Assurance)
- Receive confirmation letter (takes 4-8 weeks)
- **Important:** Get this BEFORE approaching investors

**Step 2: Structure Investment (Weeks 9-12)**
- Create shareholders' agreement
- Set valuation (typically £500k-£2m pre-money for SEIS)
- Determine equity offered (10-30% typical for £50k-£250k)
- Legal docs: Subscription agreement, Articles of Association update

**Step 3: Find SEIS Investors (Weeks 12-24)**
- **Angel networks:**
  - UK Business Angels Association (UKBAA)
  - Angel Investment Network (AIN)
  - SyndicateRoom
  - Seedrs (equity crowdfunding)
- **LinkedIn outreach:** Healthcare investors, tech angels
- **Pitch events:** Startup competitions, demo days
- **Warm introductions:** Ask network for investor connections

**Step 4: Pitch & Close (Weeks 20-26)**
- Send pitch deck to investors
- 1-to-1 meetings (typically 5-10 meetings per investor)
- Due diligence (investors review financials, product, team)
- Negotiate terms (valuation, board seats, etc.)
- Sign legal docs

**Step 5: Investment Received (Week 26+)**
- Investors transfer funds to company bank account
- Issue share certificates
- Update Companies House (file SH01)

**Step 6: SEIS Compliance Certificates (Immediately After)**
- Submit SEIS1 form to HMRC (compliance statement)
- HMRC reviews and approves
- Issue SEIS3 certificates to each investor (they claim tax relief)

### Typical SEIS Round Structure

**Pre-Money Valuation:** £500k-£1.5m (typical for pre-revenue SaaS)
**Amount Raised:** £50k-£250k
**Equity Offered:** 10-30%
**Investor Composition:**
- 5-10 angel investors (£5k-£50k each)
- OR 1-2 lead angels (£50k-£100k each)
- OR equity crowdfunding (50-200 investors at £500-£5k each)

**Example Deal:**
- Pre-money valuation: £1m
- Raise: £100k via SEIS
- Post-money valuation: £1.1m
- Equity sold: 9%
- You retain: 91%

### Timeline

**Realistic Timeline:**
- **Month 1-2:** Get HMRC Advance Assurance
- **Month 3:** Structure legal documents
- **Month 4-6:** Pitch to investors (10-20 meetings)
- **Month 7:** Close round (sign docs, receive funds)
- **Total:** 6-8 months from start to funding
