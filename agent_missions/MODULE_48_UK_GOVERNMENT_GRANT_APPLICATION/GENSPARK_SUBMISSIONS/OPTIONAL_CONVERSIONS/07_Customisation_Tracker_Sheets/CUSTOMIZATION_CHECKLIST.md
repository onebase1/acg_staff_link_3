## HELP & TROUBLESHOOTING

**Q: I don't have a Companies House number yet. What do I do?**
A: You MUST incorporate before applying for Start Up Loans. Register at https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation (£12, takes 24 hours). Come back to this once you have your registration number.

**Q: I don't have advisors recruited. Is that okay?**
A: YES. Write "To be recruited post-funding" or "In discussions". Start Up Loans understands solo founders. The advisory board section shows you've thought about gaps, which is positive.

**Q: I haven't purchased a domain name. Should I?**
A: Not required for loan application, but recommended (£10/year). If not purchased, write "To be purchased post-funding: acgstafflink.com (available for £10/year)".

**Q: My background doesn't include healthcare experience. Will that hurt my application?**
A: No. Emphasize your software development skills and willingness to learn. Mention your advisory board will include healthcare experts. Technical capability + business planning is sufficient.

**Q: Can I apply before I've recruited beta sites?**
A: YES. The business plan describes your PLAN to recruit beta sites (you'll do this with the loan funds). You don't need sites committed before applying.

**Q: What if I don't have £15k personal savings as emergency backup?**
A: That's fine - be honest. Write "No other income source - revenue projections demonstrate loan repayment capacity". The financial projections show you can repay from business revenue alone.
