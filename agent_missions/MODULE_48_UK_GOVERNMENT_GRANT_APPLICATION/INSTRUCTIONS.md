## TROUBLESHOOTING

### "I don't have a Companies House number yet"
**Solution:** Register your company first at https://www.gov.uk/register-a-company-online (£12 fee, takes 24 hours)

### "I don't have advisory board members yet"
**Solution:** Leave as placeholders in business plan. Start Up Loans doesn't require advisory board (nice to have, not mandatory). Recruit during beta testing (Month 3-6).

### "My credit score is low"
**Solution:**
1. Check your credit score (free at ClearScore, Experian)
2. If <500, work on improving before applying (pay down debts, register to vote, fix errors)
3. Start Up Loans is more flexible than banks, but very low scores (<400) may be rejected

### "I can't afford the £483/month repayment right now"
**Solution:** That's OK! Repayments start after you receive the loan (Month 1), and you'll have revenue by Month 7 (£1,000+) to cover it. The Personal Survival Budget shows you can survive until revenue starts.

### "I want to apply for £10,000 instead of £25,000"
**Solution:**
1. Adjust use of funds: £3,500 beta + £5,000 marketing + £1,500 ops = £10,000
2. Update cash flow forecast (lower starting cash, lower spend)
3. Monthly payment reduces to £193 (easier to cover)
4. Trade-off: Smaller beta (2 sites instead of 4), less marketing (fewer customers Year 1)

### "The business plan is too long (60+ pages)"
**Solution:** Start Up Loans doesn't have strict page limits. Comprehensive is better than brief. If you want to shorten:
1. Remove Appendices (keep core 11 sections)
2. Reduce competitive analysis detail
3. Shorten market analysis
4. Aim for 30-40 pages minimum
