## 8. BETA TESTING STRATEGY

### Beta Program Overview

**Purpose:** Validate product-market fit, collect metrics, generate case studies, convert 50-75% to paying customers.

**Duration:** 6 months (Month 3-8)

**Sites:** 4 total (2 NHS trusts + 2 private care homes)

**Investment:** £7,000 from grant/loan funding

---

### Beta Site Selection Criteria

**Ideal Beta Partner:**
- ✅ 50-500 staff (large enough for meaningful data, small enough to manage)
- ✅ Current pain point (high unfilled shift rate, manual processes)
- ✅ Decision-maker accessible (can get approval quickly)
- ✅ Willing to provide testimonial (case study co-marketing)
- ✅ Geographic proximity (London/South East preferred for on-site visits)

**Mix of Sites:**
- 2 NHS Staff Banks (credibility for NHS sales, public sector validation)
- 2 Private Care Homes (demonstrate versatility, private sector case studies)

---

### Beta Pilot Structure

#### Site 1: NHS Trust Staff Bank (Medium Size)

**Profile:**
- Size: 5,000-10,000 total staff, 300-500 bank staff
- Location: London or South East
- Annual bank spend: £5-15 million
- Current system: Legacy staff bank software (10+ years old, poor mobile experience)

**Recruitment Strategy:**
- LinkedIn outreach to Workforce Directors
- NHS Digital Health Networks (Healthcare IT Leaders groups)
- Personal network introductions (if available)

**Beta Offer:**
- 6 months free platform access (£3,000 value)
- Dedicated onboarding (weekly check-ins with Founder)
- Priority feature requests (NHS-specific needs)
- Co-branded case study (mutual PR benefit)

**Success Metrics:**
- Shift fill rate: Improve from 60% → 85% (25 percentage point gain)
- Time to fill: Reduce from 6 hours → 90 minutes (75% improvement)
- User adoption: 70%+ of bank staff actively using WhatsApp assistant
- Satisfaction: 80%+ NPS (Net Promoter Score)

**Timeline:**
- Week 1-2: Onboarding (import staff data, compliance docs, configure settings)
- Week 3-8: Ramp-up (train staff, promote WhatsApp assistant, monitor usage)
- Week 9-20: Steady state (collect metrics, iterate based on feedback)
- Week 21-24: Results presentation, conversion discussion

**Expected Outcome:** 75% conversion probability (NHS budget available, proven ROI)

---

#### Site 2: Private Care Home Group (Small Chain)

**Profile:**
- Size: 5-10 care homes, 200-400 total beds
- Location: South East England
- Annual staffing budget: £3-8 million
- Current system: WhatsApp groups + Excel spreadsheets (manual, non-compliant)

**Recruitment Strategy:**
- Care Home Association events
- LinkedIn outreach to Operations Directors
- Cold outreach to care home chains (website contact forms)

**Beta Offer:** Same as Site 1

**Success Metrics:**
- CQC compliance: 100% staff with current documents (vs. 85% baseline)
- Admin time: Reduce from 15 hours/week → 5 hours/week (67% reduction)
- Cost savings: £3,000-£5,000/month (reduced agency premium, less overtime)
- Satisfaction: 80%+ from care home managers

**Timeline:** Same as Site 1

**Expected Outcome:** 50% conversion probability (lower budget than NHS, but clear ROI)

---

#### Site 3: NHS Trust Staff Bank (Large Size)

**Profile:**
- Size: 15,000+ total staff, 800-1,200 bank staff
- Location: London (tertiary hospital - teaching trust)
- Annual bank spend: £30-50 million
- Current system: Allocate HealthRoster or similar (expensive, desktop-focused)

**Recruitment Strategy:**
- NHS Innovation Accelerator referrals
- Academic Health Science Networks (AHSNs)
- Direct approach via procurement team

**Beta Offer:** Same as Site 1 + exclusive "Reference Customer" status

**Success Metrics:**
- Same as Site 1, but larger scale (more data points, statistical significance)
- Media opportunity: Press release ("Major London Teaching Hospital Pilots AI Staffing")

**Timeline:** Same as Site 1

**Expected Outcome:** 60% conversion probability (longer procurement, but high-value reference customer)

---

#### Site 4: Private Care Home (Single Site or Small Chain)

**Profile:**
- Size: 2-3 care homes, 100-200 beds
- Location: South East England
- Annual staffing budget: £1-3 million
- Current system: Manual (phone calls, paper timesheets)

**Recruitment Strategy:**
- Local care home associations
- Direct outreach (cold calls/emails)
- Referrals from Site 2 (if positive experience)

**Beta Offer:** Same as Site 1

**Success Metrics:**
- Focus on ease of use (less tech-savvy users)
- Timesheet accuracy: Improve from 70% → 95% (OCR reduces errors)
- Invoice speed: 5 days → 24 hours (cash flow improvement)

**Timeline:** Same as Site 1

**Expected Outcome:** 50% conversion probability (smaller budget, but high ROI)

### Data Collection & Metrics

**Automated Metrics (Platform Analytics):**
- Shift posted → filled time (average, median, P90)
- Shift fill rate (% filled vs. posted)
- User adoption (% staff using WhatsApp vs. web vs. not using)
- Compliance status (% staff with current documents)
- Timesheet processing time (upload → approved average)
- Invoice generation time (shift end → invoice sent)

**Survey Metrics (Quarterly):**
- Net Promoter Score (NPS): "How likely are you to recommend ACG StaffLink?" (0-10 scale)
- Feature satisfaction: Rate each feature 1-5 stars
- Pain point resolution: "Has ACG StaffLink solved your biggest staffing challenge?"
- Improvement requests: Open-ended feedback

**Financial Metrics (Provided by Beta Site):**
- Cost savings (admin hours reduced × £25/hour)
- Revenue impact (fewer unfilled shifts × avg shift value)
- ROI calculation (savings - software cost)

---

### Beta Success Criteria

**Minimum Viable Success (Required to Proceed to Paid Launch):**
- ✅ At least 2/4 sites achieve 70%+ user adoption
- ✅ At least 3/4 sites report "Very Satisfied" or "Satisfied" overall
- ✅ At least 1 site commits to paying customer conversion
- ✅ Zero critical bugs or security incidents
- ✅ GDPR/CQC compliance verified by at least 1 NHS trust

**Ideal Success (Strong Market Validation):**
- ✅ All 4 sites achieve 80%+ user adoption
- ✅ All 4 sites report measurable ROI (£3k+ savings/month)
- ✅ 3-4 sites convert to paying customers (75%+ conversion rate)
- ✅ At least 2 sites agree to video testimonial
- ✅ Metrics demonstrate 50%+ improvement in key KPIs (fill rate, time to fill)

---

### Risk Mitigation

**Risk 1: Beta Site Drops Out**
- **Mitigation:** Recruit 5 sites, expect 1 dropout (80% completion rate)
- **Backup:** Have 2-3 waitlist sites ready to replace dropouts

**Risk 2: Low User Adoption**
- **Mitigation:** Weekly engagement campaigns (WhatsApp tips, success stories)
- **Mitigation:** On-site training sessions (if needed)
- **Pivot:** If WhatsApp adoption low, provide web/mobile app alternative

**Risk 3: Technical Issues During Beta**
- **Mitigation:** 24/7 monitoring, <4h response time for critical bugs
- **Mitigation:** Staging environment for testing before production releases
- **Contingency:** £1,500 budget for emergency freelance dev support

**Risk 4: No Sites Convert to Paid**
- **Mitigation:** Monthly ROI dashboards (show value continuously, not just at end)
- **Mitigation:** Extend beta by 2 months if needed (Month 9-10) to prove value
- **Pivot:** If beta fails, accelerate warm pipeline outreach (30 agencies)

---

### Beta Deliverables (End of Month 8)

**For Grant/Loan Reporting:**
- ✅ Beta completion report (metrics, outcomes, lessons learned)
- ✅ 4 case studies (PDF + blog post format)
- ✅ Video testimonials (2-4 sites, depending on willingness)
- ✅ ROI calculator (based on actual beta data)
- ✅ Product roadmap (updated based on beta feedback)

**For Sales & Marketing:**
- ✅ Reference customers (beta sites willing to take calls from prospects)
- ✅ Metrics infographic (visual summary of improvements)
- ✅ Press release (if major NHS trust or impressive results)
- ✅ Website testimonials page (quotes + logos)

**For Investors (Future SEIS Raise):**
- ✅ Product-market fit validation (beta results prove demand)
- ✅ Customer acquisition cost (CAC) data (beta sites → paid customers)
- ✅ Retention data (Month 7-12 churn rate from beta converts)

**BETA TESTING STRATEGY SUMMARY:**

The 6-month beta program with **4 sites (2 NHS + 2 private)** will validate product-market fit, generate case studies, and convert **2-3 sites to paying customers**.

**£7,000 investment** generates:
- ✅ Market validation (independent proof platform works)

**ROI:** £7,000 → £10,800 ARR + marketing assets (1.5x direct return + invaluable credibility)
