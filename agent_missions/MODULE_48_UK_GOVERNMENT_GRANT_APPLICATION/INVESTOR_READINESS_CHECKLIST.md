# INVESTOR READINESS CHECKLIST
## The Path from £25k Loan to £500k Series Seed

**🔒 CONFIDENTIAL - Internal Strategy Document**
**DO NOT INCLUDE IN GRANT APPLICATIONS**

---

## PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT

This checklist tracks your journey from **pre-revenue startup** (Month 1) to **investor-ready company** (Month 12-15) capable of raising £100k-£250k SEIS or £500k+ Series Seed.

**The Strategy:**
1. **Grant/Loan (£25k)** → Validate product-market fit via beta
2. **Beta Success** → Convert 2-3 sites to paying customers
3. **Warm Pipeline** → Close 15-20 customers from 30-agency network
4. **Traction** → Hit £50k-£120k ARR by Month 12
5. **SEIS Raise (£100k-£250k)** → Scale marketing, hire team
6. **Series Seed (£500k+)** → Achieve market leadership

---

## MONTH-BY-MONTH ROADMAP

### MONTH 1-2: SECURE LOAN & FOUNDATION

**Funding:**
- [ ] Submit Start Up Loans application (business plan, cash flow, personal budget)
- [ ] Receive loan approval (£25,000 at 6%)
- [ ] Funds deposited in business account

**Legal & Compliance:**
- [ ] Register with ICO as data controller (£40)
- [ ] Draft customer contract templates (DPA, T&Cs, MSA)
- [ ] Purchase Professional Indemnity Insurance (£1m cover)
- [ ] Purchase Cyber Insurance (£500k cover)

**Marketing Setup:**
- [ ] Create LinkedIn company page
- [ ] Post 3x/week on LinkedIn (establish thought leadership)
- [ ] Build email list (sign up form on website)
- [ ] Draft beta program recruitment emails

**Metrics to Track:**
- Cash balance: £25,000
- LinkedIn followers: 100+
- Email list: 50+ contacts

**Beta Recruitment:**
- [ ] Identify 5 potential beta sites (target: 2 NHS + 2 care homes + 1 backup)

- [ ] Onboard Site 1 (NHS Trust) - Week 1-2

**Marketing:**

**Metrics to Track:**

## KEY METRICS TO TRACK (MONTHLY)

### Financial Metrics
- [ ] MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
- [ ] ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)
- [ ] Revenue growth rate (MoM %)
- [ ] Cash balance
- [ ] Burn rate (monthly spend - revenue)
- [ ] Runway (months of cash remaining)

### Customer Metrics
- [ ] Total customers (paying)
- [ ] New customers (this month)
- [ ] Churned customers (this month)
- [ ] Net new customers (new - churned)
- [ ] Customer concentration (% revenue from top 3 customers)

### Unit Economics
- [ ] CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
- [ ] LTV (Lifetime Value)
- [ ] LTV:CAC ratio (target: 3:1 minimum, 10:1+ excellent)
- [ ] Payback period (months to recover CAC)
- [ ] Gross margin (%)

### Engagement Metrics
- [ ] Monthly Active Users (MAU) - staff using platform
- [ ] WhatsApp message volume (engagement signal)
- [ ] Shifts posted per customer (usage depth)
- [ ] NPS (Net Promoter Score - quarterly survey)

### Sales Metrics
- [ ] Pipeline value (potential ARR from prospects)
- [ ] Demo-to-trial conversion rate (target: 20%+)
- [ ] Trial-to-paid conversion rate (target: 50%+)
- [ ] Sales cycle length (days from first contact to close)
- [ ] Win rate (deals won ÷ deals pursued)

## INVESTOR DUE DILIGENCE CHECKLIST

**When investors say "We're interested," they'll request:**

### Financial Due Diligence
- [ ] Historical financial statements (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow)
- [ ] Management accounts (monthly breakdown)
- [ ] Customer contracts (redacted, sample of 5-10)
- [ ] Revenue breakdown by customer (anonymized)
- [ ] CAC/LTV calculation methodology
- [ ] Financial model (3-5 year projections)
- [ ] Cap table (current ownership structure)

### Legal Due Diligence
- [ ] Certificate of incorporation
- [ ] Articles of association
- [ ] Shareholder agreements (if any)
- [ ] IP assignment agreements (confirm company owns code)
- [ ] Customer contracts (template + samples)
- [ ] Data Processing Agreements (GDPR compliance)
- [ ] Employment contracts (if any team members)
- [ ] Insurance policies (PI, cyber, key man if applicable)

### Product Due Diligence
- [ ] Platform demo (live walkthrough)
- [ ] Technical architecture document
- [ ] Security & compliance documentation (GDPR, NHS DSPT)
- [ ] Product roadmap (6-12 month plan)
- [ ] Customer feedback/testimonials
- [ ] Case studies (beta results)

### Commercial Due Diligence
- [ ] Customer list (anonymized, with ARR per customer)
- [ ] Churn analysis (reasons for cancellations)
- [ ] Pipeline report (sales forecast)
- [ ] Market research (TAM, SAM, SOM calculations)
- [ ] Competitive analysis
- [ ] Reference calls (3-5 customers willing to speak to investors)

### Team Due Diligence
- [ ] Founder CV/bio
- [ ] Team org chart
- [ ] Advisory board bios (if applicable)
- [ ] Hiring plan (roles, timeline, budget)

## SUCCESS MILESTONES

### MONTH 6 (END OF BETA)
✅ **Milestone:** 4 beta sites validated product-market fit
- Criteria: 80%+ NPS, 50%+ improvement in key metrics
- Deliverables: 4 case studies, 2 video testimonials
- Decision point: If <2 sites satisfied, pivot strategy

### MONTH 8 (FIRST CUSTOMERS)
✅ **Milestone:** 2-3 paying customers from beta conversions
- Criteria: £600-£1,500 MRR, <10% churn
- Deliverables: First revenue, proof of willingness to pay
- Decision point: If 0 conversions, diagnose problem (pricing? value prop? product?)

### MONTH 12 (INVESTOR READINESS)
✅ **Milestone:** £72k-£120k ARR, 15-20 customers
- Criteria: 30% MoM growth, £500 CAC, 20:1 LTV:CAC
- Deliverables: Pitch deck, data room, HMRC SEIS approval
- Decision point: If <£50k ARR, delay fundraise 3-6 months

### MONTH 15 (SEIS CLOSED)
✅ **Milestone:** £100k-£150k raised, 85-90% founder ownership retained
- Criteria: Lead investor + 3-5 angels, pre-money £1-1.5m valuation
- Deliverables: Cash in bank, investor updates plan
- Decision point: If no term sheets, bootstrap or pivot to profitability focus

### MONTH 24 (SERIES SEED READINESS)
✅ **Milestone:** £500k ARR run rate, 100+ customers
- Criteria: Sustainable growth (40%+ MoM), proven team execution
- Deliverables: VC pitch deck, £3-5m valuation justification
- Decision point: Raise Series Seed OR continue bootstrapping to profitability

## RISK MANAGEMENT

### Risk 1: Beta Fails (No Sites Convert)
**Indicators:** Month 8, 0 paying customers
**Action:** Pivot to warm pipeline aggressively (30 agencies), offer extended trial (3 months free)

### Risk 2: Slow Customer Acquisition
**Indicators:** Month 10, <5 customers
**Action:** Reduce burn (cut marketing 50%), extend runway, focus on profitability not growth

### Risk 3: High Churn
**Indicators:** Month 12, >15% monthly churn
**Action:** Pause new customer acquisition, fix product issues, implement customer success program

### Risk 4: Can't Raise SEIS
**Indicators:** Month 13-15, no investor interest
**Action:** Bootstrap to profitability (hire freeze, grow organically), try again at £200k+ ARR

### Risk 5: Cash Shortage
**Indicators:** Cash balance <£5,000
**Action:** Emergency measures (cut all non-essential spend, founder takes contract work, bridge loan from F&F)

## FINAL CHECKLIST: AM I INVESTOR-READY?

**Before pitching SEIS investors, ensure:**

- [ ] **Traction:** £50k+ ARR minimum, ideally £72k-£120k
- [ ] **Growth:** 20-30% MoM revenue growth (consistent 3+ months)
- [ ] **Customers:** 15-20 paying customers (diversified, not 1-2 large)
- [ ] **Unit Economics:** CAC <£1,000, LTV >£5,000, LTV:CAC >5:1
- [ ] **Retention:** Churn <10% monthly, NPS >50
- [ ] **Founder:** Full-time commitment, compelling story, coachable
- [ ] **Market:** Large TAM (£1bn+), clear growth opportunity
- [ ] **Pitch:** 15-slide deck, 10-minute delivery, answers all objections
- [ ] **Data Room:** All docs ready (financial model, contracts, legal, metrics)
- [ ] **SEIS:** HMRC Advance Assurance received (2-3 months process)

**If <5 boxes checked:** Not ready. Keep building traction for 3-6 months.
**If 5-8 boxes checked:** Borderline. Test with 2-3 angels, get feedback.
**If 9-10 boxes checked:** GO! You're investor-ready. Start outreach.
