## 6. PRODUCT & SERVICE LINE

### Product Overview

**ACG StaffLink is a cloud-based Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform** that automates the entire healthcare staffing lifecycle - from shift posting to invoicing - using AI and modern automation.

### Core Product Features

#### 1. AI-Powered WhatsApp Assistant

**The Unique Differentiator**

Healthcare professionals can interact with the platform using natural language via WhatsApp:

**Example Conversation:**
```
Staff Member (via WhatsApp): "Hi, show me night shifts this weekend"

ACG StaffLink AI: "Found 3 night shifts available this weekend:

1. 🏥 St Mary's Hospital, RN, Sat 7pm-7am, £280
2. 🏥 City Care Home, HCA, Sat 10pm-8am, £120
3. 🏥 Green Acres, RN, Sun 7pm-7am, £280

Reply with the number to view details or 'apply 1' to book shift 1."

Staff Member: "apply 1"

ACG StaffLink AI: "✅ Shift booked! You're confirmed for:
St Mary's Hospital, RN, Saturday 19:00-07:00
Address: 123 Hospital Rd, London
Contact: Ward Sister Jane (07xxx)

I've sent shift details to your email. Good luck! 👍"
```

**Technical Implementation:**
- WhatsApp Business API (official Facebook integration)
- OpenAI GPT-4 (natural language understanding)
- Intent detection (booking, availability query, shift details)
- Context awareness (remembers conversation history)
- Multi-language support (future: Polish, Romanian for EU staff)

**Benefits:**
- ✅ 95% smartphone penetration (everyone has WhatsApp)
- ✅ No app download required (instant adoption)
- ✅ Familiar interface (78% healthcare workers use WhatsApp daily)
- ✅ 24/7 availability (AI never sleeps)
- ✅ Reduces admin phone calls 80%+

---

#### 2. Intelligent Shift Matching

**Automated Staff-to-Shift Matching**

When a shift is posted, the platform automatically:

1. **Filters qualified staff:**
   - Role match (RN, HCA, Doctor, etc.)
   - Location proximity (within X miles)
   - Availability (not already booked)
   - Compliance status (DBS, training, registration current)

2. **Ranks by fit score:**
   - Previous shifts at same facility (familiar = higher quality)
   - Specialty match (dementia ward → dementia-trained staff)
   - Performance rating (5-star system from previous shifts)
   - Preference match (night shift preference for night shifts)

3. **Notifies top matches:**
   - WhatsApp message to top 5 qualified staff
   - Email backup (if WhatsApp not acknowledged in 5 minutes)
   - SMS fallback (if no response in 15 minutes)

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Fill shifts 70% faster (4 hours → 60 minutes)
- ✅ Higher quality matches (compliance guaranteed, preference-based)
- ✅ Reduced no-shows (familiar facilities = better engagement)
- ✅ Fair distribution (prevents favorites, spreads shifts equally)

---

#### 3. Compliance Tracking & Automation

**CQC-Ready Document Management**

Healthcare staffing requires extensive compliance documentation:

**Staff Documents Tracked:**
- DBS Certificate (Disclosure and Barring Service - criminal record check)
- Professional Registration (NMC for nurses, HCPC for allied health, GMC for doctors)
- Right to Work (passport, visa, settled status)
- Mandatory Training (10+ certificates: manual handling, safeguarding, fire safety, etc.)
- Vaccination Records (COVID-19, Hepatitis B, MMR)
- Driving License (if role requires driving)
- References (2 professional, 1 character)
- Occupational Health Clearance

**Automated Features:**
- **OCR Document Reading:** Upload PDF/photo → AI extracts expiry date
- **Expiry Monitoring:** Dashboard shows documents expiring in 30/14/7 days
- **Auto-Reminders:** WhatsApp/email/SMS reminders to staff before expiry
- **Shift Blocking:** Cannot be booked for shifts if any document expired
- **CQC Audit Export:** One-click download of all compliance docs (ZIP file)

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Zero compliance breaches (automated blocking)
- ✅ 90% reduction in admin time (manual spreadsheet tracking eliminated)
- ✅ CQC audit-ready (complete audit trail, instant export)
- ✅ Staff accountability (automated reminders prevent last-minute panic)

---

The platform sends notifications via 3 channels (with automatic fallback):

**Channel Priority:**
1. **WhatsApp** (fastest, highest engagement - 90% read within 5 minutes)

**Notification Types:**
- **For Staff:**
  - New shift matching your profile

  - Payment confirmed

- **For Agencies:**

  - System health alerts

**Benefits:**
- ✅ 99.9% delivery rate (triple redundancy)
- ✅ Reduced no-shows (timely reminders)
- ✅ Faster response times (WhatsApp instant vs email delayed)

---

#### 5. Timesheet Processing & OCR

**Automate Timesheet Approval**

**Current Pain:** Agencies receive paper timesheets (scanned/photos), manually transcribe hours, calculate pay.

**ACG StaffLink Solution:**

1. **Staff uploads timesheet photo** (via WhatsApp or app)
2. **OCR extracts data:**
   - Shift date and times (start/end)
   - Break duration
   - Staff name and signature
   - Client/ward signature (approval)
3. **AI validates:**
   - Cross-check against booked shift (dates match?)
   - Calculate hours (accounting for breaks)
   - Flag anomalies (overtime, early finish, no signature)
4. **Admin reviews flagged items only** (90% auto-approved)
5. **Invoice auto-generated** (approved timesheets → invoice)

**Benefits:**
- ✅ 80% reduction in timesheet processing time (3-5 days → 4-6 hours)
- ✅ Fewer errors (OCR + validation vs manual transcription)
- ✅ Faster payment cycles (staff paid weekly, not bi-weekly)

---

#### 6. Automated Invoicing

**From Shift Completion to Payment in 24 Hours**

**Workflow:**
1. Shift completes → timesheet uploaded → OCR extracts hours
2. Admin approves (or auto-approve if no flags)
3. **Invoice auto-generated:**
   - Professional branded PDF (agency logo, colors)
   - Itemized breakdown (hours × rate)
   - VAT calculation (if applicable)
   - Payment terms (Net 30, BACS details)
4. **Invoice emailed to client** (automated via Resend API)
5. **Payment tracking:**
   - Dashboard shows: Sent, Viewed (email opened), Paid
   - Auto-reminders at 7, 14, 21 days if unpaid
6. **Reconciliation:**
   - Mark as paid manually or via bank feed integration (future feature)

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Cash flow improvement (invoice sent within 24h vs 3-5 days)
- ✅ Professional image (branded invoices vs Excel)
- ✅ Payment tracking visibility (know which clients are slow-paying)

---

#### 7. Real-Time Analytics Dashboard

**Business Intelligence for Healthcare Staffing**

**Agency Owner Dashboard shows:**
- **Shift Metrics:**
  - Total shifts posted (week/month/year)
  - Fill rate (85% target)
  - Average time to fill (60 minutes target)
  - Cancellation rate (track unreliable staff)

- **Staff Metrics:**
  - Total active staff (compliance-current)
  - Top performers (5-star ratings, high acceptance rate)
  - At-risk staff (documents expiring soon)
  - Utilization rate (shifts worked vs available)

- **Financial Metrics:**
  - Revenue (invoiced vs paid)
  - Outstanding invoices (aging report)
  - Profit margin per shift (bill rate - pay rate)
  - Monthly recurring revenue (MRR trend)

- **Compliance Metrics:**
  - Documents expiring (30/14/7 day alerts)
  - Compliance breach incidents (zero tolerance target)
  - Audit readiness score (100% target)

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Data-driven decisions (hire more HCAs vs RNs based on demand)
- ✅ Identify at-risk clients (slow payment → credit terms tightening)
- ✅ Performance management (recognize top staff, address underperformers)

### Pricing Strategy

- **Time saved:** 20 hours/week (shift booking 10h, compliance 5h, invoicing 5h)

- **Software cost:** £299-£499/month
- **Net savings:** £1,500-£1,700/month (3-6x ROI)

**Objection Handling:**
- **"Too expensive":** "You're currently spending £2,000/month on admin labor. We save you £1,500/month - that's a 5x return."
- **"Not sure we need it":** "Let's run a free trial. If you don't save at least 10 hours in the first month, we'll refund you."
- **"Can we pay annually?":** "Yes - 12 months for the price of 10 (£2,990 vs £3,588)" [creates cash flow, reduces churn]
