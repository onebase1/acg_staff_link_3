## 🚀 1. Executive Summary (The Elevator Pitch)
ACG StaffLink is a revolutionary **Agentic ERP** designed to solve the critical staffing crisis in the UK healthcare sector. Unlike traditional management software which requires manual data entry and constant human intervention, ACG StaffLink embeds an autonomous AI Core (n8n + Gemini) directly into the agency's workflow. 

Our vision is a **"Zero-Admin" agency** where the system automatically handles 99% of scheduling, compliance, and messaging, allowing healthcare operators to focus on resident care rather than administrative logistics. We are currently in pilot with 4 agencies in the North East, proving a 20% reduction in operational overhead.

## 🛠️ 2. Technical Innovation Statement (R&D Proof)
The core innovation of ACG StaffLink lies in its **Asynchronous Agentic Architecture**. 

*   **Technology Stack**: Built on a high-availability Supabase backend with high-speed Edge Functions, orchestrated by an n8n AI engine.
*   **The "Kylie" Core**: Our proprietary AI Voice Receptionist capable of handling incoming sickness calls, verifying staff identity, and autonomously triggering shift-replacements in real-time.
*   **Agentic Compliance**: A "Self-Healing" compliance engine that continuously monitors DBS and training expiry, automatically suspending accounts and notifying staff via WhatsApp without human manager input.
*   **Interoperability**: Designed to consolidate the functionality of Cama, BrightHR, and WhatsApp into a single, encrypted source of truth.
